# Farmer post

Serves #37 (more touch points: "reminders, small snippets of technical information"), #41 (social, not email or text) and #42 (information, not selling).

## Inputs
- The source: a line Chris said or wrote, a slide, a report line, a page on the business website or the project website, or an approved flyer in `AI Team/workshops/<YYYY-MM-DD>-<town>/`. The project website is part of a project the business manages, not Chris's own company, so a source taken from it is the project's material.
- `owner.md`: the project, the funder acknowledgement line if the project has one, the photo rule, and the line that names the social platforms (if it says ASK or is empty, Chris hasn't decided: #39).
- `post-log.md`: what has gone out lately and what Chris held or changed. Master copy `AI Team/projects/_content/post-log.md`, saved by Adrielle.
- `seasonal-calendar.md`, when the post is a reminder.

## Steps
1. **Find the source.** Every fact in the post must trace to something Chris said, wrote or approved. If there is none, stop and ask one question: "What's the one thing you'd want a farmer to know about <topic>?" Never fill a gap from general knowledge.
2. **Check `post-log.md`.** Don't repeat a recent topic or re-offer one Chris held. If the file can't be read, say "recent posts UNKNOWN" and carry on. Never say "no repeat".
3. **Pick one idea** and one type: a reminder (do this, by when, why), a snippet (one fact, why it matters), what's new (one finding, with the link), or a workshop notice (what, when, where, how to come, using the approved flyer wording and nothing new). A follow-up after a workshop waits until Chris has answered #35 and Workshops & Travel has handed over key points. Never name or quote a participant.
4. **Write two versions** in plain words farmers use, Australian spelling, calm tone, no hype, no scare, no emoji unless Chris's own posts use them. Short: 280 characters or fewer. Standard: 600 characters or fewer, with the useful detail. Numbers and units exactly as the source has them.
5. **Chemicals and fumigation.** Don't name a product, rate or dose unless it is copied word for word from Chris's source. If you do, tag it `CHECK: label` so Chris confirms it against the label.
6. **Stay platform-neutral** until `owner.md` names the platforms: no hashtags, handles, polls or platform features. Once it does, add one line per platform with its length and where the link goes.
7. **Link and picture.** Give the page it points to (say whether you saw it live) and one picture idea: Chris's photo or diagram, the social image from the flyer, or a Canva template for Adrielle. Photos of people need their OK.
8. **Funder line.** If the project's entry in `owner.md` requires an acknowledgement, copy it exactly. The check always applies when the source or the link is the project website, because that material is the project's. If `owner.md` doesn't say, write `FUNDER LINE: ask Chris`. Never name a funder from memory.
9. **Inform-only check.** The draft has no price, offer, discount, "book now", "get in touch for a quote", or claim that the business is best. A workshop notice says how to attend and nothing more.
10. **Hand over** for Adrielle to send to Chris on Teams: "Approve or change?" (SOP 27). Do not wait for the answer.

## Output
```
POST DRAFT <n> · <type> · <project from owner.md, or general>
Short: ...
Standard: ...
Link: <page> (seen live: yes/no)    Picture: ...
Funder line: ...    Source: <what Chris said or wrote, and where>
Checks: source PASS · one idea PASS · inform-only PASS · label n/a · platform-neutral PASS
For Chris: Approve or change?
After posting, Adrielle pastes back the final text. Post-log line: <date> | <platform> | <topic> | <one-line gist> | posted/held/changed
```

## Done when
Chris has a draft with a source line and every check marked, and it is waiting for his yes. After Adrielle posts, the post-log line is ready for her to save.

## Never
- Post, schedule, reply to comments or log in to an account.
- Sell, pitch or ask for business.
- Invent or improve a grain-storage fact, number, product, rate or fumigation detail, or write a quote or testimonial.
- Name a funder, or a person, from memory.
- Use a platform-specific feature before Chris has chosen platforms.
- Propose an email or text campaign to farmers.
