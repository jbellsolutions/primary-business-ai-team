# Site health check

Serves #43 (keep the site tidy: archiving, broken links) and, through the social-link check, #38 ("all our links are down"). Follows SOP 26 (Website upkeep).

## Inputs
- The public pages of each website, as far as the desk can open them. If it can't open a page, Adrielle pastes the page text or a screenshot description. That page is UNKNOWN until then.
- A link-checker report from this month, if Adrielle has run one (SOP 26 step 3; which tool is `check with Chris`).
- The workshop folder names under `AI Team/workshops/` (date and town) and the website events on the site, to compare.
- `owner.md`: who manages each site and where its domain and hosting sit (open: #92).
- Last month's list, if there is one.

## Steps
1. **One site at a time.** Read it as a visitor.
   - Look at the home page, menus, events page, latest articles and contact page, and what the menus lead to.
   - Note how many pages you opened out of how many you could see.
2. **Events.** List every event whose date has passed (archive candidates: title, date, link). For upcoming events, compare date, time and venue with the workshop folder. A mismatch goes to Adrielle, to pass to Workshops & Travel. This desk doesn't edit events.
3. **Links.** From the link-checker report, list each broken link: page · link text · where it goes · the error · what it should be (only if you can show it). With no report, check the links you can open and say "link check not run; <n> of <m> links tried". Never write "no broken links" from a sample.
4. **Out of date.** Pages that name a past season, a past date, a person or contact that may have changed, or "coming soon". These are for Chris to review, not for deleting.
   - The project website is the project's, not Chris's own company, so also note any article or page there that lacks the funder acknowledgement line from `owner.md`, as "funder line missing?" for Chris. If `owner.md` has no line, write `FUNDER LINE: ask Chris` once.
5. **Social links.** List where each social link on the site goes and whether it opens the right account. Compare with the setup plan if there is one.
6. **Not Adrielle's to fix.** Layout, forms, plugin errors, a slow or down site, hosting, domain or security notices: list what you saw and on which page, for the person who manages the site (per `owner.md`; if unknown, ask Chris who) and for Chris. A message to that person goes on Chris's end-of-day "Needs your yes" list, and Adrielle sends it only after his yes, or when `AI Team/context/standing-approvals.md` covers that exact template (if that file can't be read, treat it as not covered).
7. **Sort and cap.**
   - P1: public and wrong, such as an upcoming event with the wrong detail, a dead link on a main page, or a form that fails.
   - P2: broken links elsewhere, and past events to archive.
   - P3: tidying.
   List the 20 most important and say how many more there are.
8. **Compare with last month:** fixed, new, still there.
9. **Draft the log line** for SOP 26 step 8: date · site · archived · fixed · reported.

## Output
For each site: a table (priority · page · what · who acts), then three short lists: For Adrielle to do in WordPress; For the site manager; For Chris to decide. Then the log line.

## Done when
Every item names a page, what is wrong and who acts, and anything not checked is marked UNKNOWN.

## Never
- Log in, click through admin, or fix anything.
- Delete. Old events and pages are archived by a person.
- Touch themes, plugins, menus, settings, hosting or the domain.
- Say "all clear" after a partial check.
