# Social setup plan

Serves #38 (one integrated presence with several users, not a free single-user account: "I want to get it right") and #39 (which platforms, and who holds the log-ins, is open). Not urgent, and Chris decides.

## Inputs
- `owner.md`: platforms and existing accounts, and who holds the log-ins (probably still ASK).
- What exists today, which Adrielle lists by looking at each account. She never pastes a password.
- The social links on the business website and the project website (from site-health-check).
- SOP 27 (Social post) and SOP 30 (New starter and leaver access checklist).

## Steps
1. **Start from the need.** Write the requirements in Chris's terms and ask him to confirm them: the business owns the account, not a person's own log-in; each person (Chris, Adrielle) signs in as themselves; Chris can add and remove people; Chris approves before anything is posted; farmers can find the account from the websites; the links work; the platform suits short reminders and snippets; the cost is known and Chris has said yes.
2. **Inventory, which becomes the account register.** One table, with these columns:
   - platform, account name, who made it, and whether it is a personal or a business account;
   - roles, and who holds each (names only, never a password);
   - the password-manager item name (the name only), and whether it can take more than one user;
   - linked from the sites, and whether the link works.
   Anything not seen is UNKNOWN.
3. **Platforms (#39).** Don't pick for Chris. Give him a decision card for each candidate he or Adrielle names: are his farmers on it; does it allow a business account with separate roles; does it suit short posts and pictures; what does it cost. Offer one way to get evidence: add "Which of these do you use?" to the next workshop evaluation, if Chris says yes (it goes to Workshops & Travel).
4. **Setup checklist** for Chris to approve, then Adrielle to do:
   - the business owns each account, created with an address the business controls (`check with Chris: which`);
   - Chris is the owner or admin, and Adrielle has the role that lets her draft and post but not remove him;
   - every user has two-step sign-in, and Chris holds the recovery contacts;
   - log-ins that must be stored are shared per person through the password manager by Chris. No shared passwords, and this desk never sees the vault;
   - profile basics: name, short description, link to the site, a profile picture from Canva, one pinned post;
   - fix the links: every social link on both sites and in email signatures goes to the new account;
   - comments: they go to Chris (SOP 27 step 8).
5. **The old free account.** Offer the options (keep it with a "we've moved" post, or close it later) and let Chris choose. Never close or delete it.
6. **Platform mechanics** (role names, prices, features) change. Write "verify on the platform's own help pages at set-up" next to each. Don't state them from memory.
7. **Ask Chris one decision at a time:** which platforms; which business address; who is owner; the budget; the old account.
8. **After he decides,** Adrielle saves the platform line in `owner.md`, and farmer-post adds a line per platform. Scheduling tools wait for the later Social Publisher desk.

## Output
One page: requirements, the account register, decision cards, checklist, link-fix list, and the next single decision for Chris. Adrielle saves the register; the later Social Publisher desk reads it.

## Done when
Chris has the one-page plan and has answered the platform question, or it is on his list with the decisions still open. Nothing has been created.

## Never
- Create an account, log in, or take or store a password, code or key.
- Suggest one shared log-in for the team.
- Close, delete or merge an account, or buy a tool.
- Recommend a platform as if the facts were known.
