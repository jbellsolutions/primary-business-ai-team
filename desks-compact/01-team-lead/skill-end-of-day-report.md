# End-of-day report

Serves asks #14 and #91. Follow SOP 07.

## Inputs
- Todoist after Adrielle has ticked and commented: done, in progress, waiting. Read through the app, or she pastes it.
- The day plan from the morning and her notes.
- The status lines the day's scheduled tasks produced, which Adrielle pastes in.
- Drafts waiting on Chris from every desk, as she pastes them: flyers, replies, posts, bookings, NAB payments prepared for him to authorise, SOP drafts.
- Chris's calendar for tomorrow.
- `owner.md`: how Chris likes the report delivered (Teams or email: check with Chris) and whether he still wants time logged (Clockify is ask #84, still open).
- `routines-on.md`: which routines are on.

## Steps
1. Ask Adrielle to tick what is done and comment on what is in progress (SOP 07 step 1). Then compare today's plan with Todoist.
2. **Needs your yes** goes first. Put every approval or authorisation waiting on Chris in one numbered list. Each line gives the item, the exact words he must give (yes, pick one, or an answer), and where he can see it. This is the single approvals list the package promises (#91). Remove anything already approved.
3. Then write **Done today**, **In progress and waiting**, **Problems or questions**, and **Tomorrow's top three**.
4. Each Done line shows proof: a ticked task, a saved file or a sent item. If a job isn't done, say why in one honest line. This is a report, not a diary (SOP 07).
5. Time: if Chris still wants it, write "Time logged: yes or no", in Adrielle's own sign-in. Never work the figures out.
6. Status lines: put the pasted lines in time order. Compare with `routines-on.md`. A routine that is on, was due today and has no line is "no line found: did it run?". Never call that zero.
7. Write the Daily note for `AI Team/notebook/Daily/<YYYY-MM-DD>.md`. It uses Obsidian-compatible markdown and links back to [[Home]] and, if a decision was made today, to [[Decisions]]. Its sections are Plan, Done, Waiting on Chris, Tomorrow, and Status lines.
8. Check (#66, SOP 07 step 5): every Todoist task touched today is in the report or explained. Every "Needs your yes" line is still open. The counts agree.

## Output
In this order:
1. The message for Chris, one screen, ready for Teams.
2. The run-log block, to append to `AI Team/log/runs-<YYYY-MM>.md`. It holds one-line results only, never message contents.
3. The Daily note.
4. Up to three lines for tomorrow's catch-up.

Adrielle checks every line and sends or saves each one herself.

## Done when
Chris can see on one screen what is done, what needs him and what is next. The log block and the Daily note are ready to save.

## Never
- Send, save, tick or close anything.
- Say "done" without proof, or hide a problem.
- Spread approvals through the report. They live in one list at the top.
- Put participant details, bank details or message contents in the report, the note or the log.
- Mark an SOP approved, or a routine on.
